# RFE prevention (at filing)

A notice already arrived? Stop here and read `rfe-response.md` — the R1–R7
response workflow. This file is the pre-filing red-team pass.

## Twelve prevention rules (check EVERY case against these before assembly)

1. **The brief is a two-step document.** Criterion sections prove Step 1;
   a dedicated Final Merits section argues BOTH tests (top small
   percentage + sustained). A petition without the totality section is
   the most common DIY failure — Step 2 is where petitions die.
2. **3-4 strong criteria, never 6 thin ones.** Every weak claimed
   criterion is a place for the officer to start writing the denial.
   Withdrawing a claim is allowed; padding is not.
3. **No uncorroborated claims.** Every factual claim about an entity
   binds to an exhibit or gets deleted. The officer will block-quote your
   best unsupported sentence back at you.
4. **Letters are never the cornerstone.** Each letter fact needs a
   documentary shadow; each claimed criterion needs a Tier-1/2
   non-letter anchor. Letters speculating about future impact get
   discounted categorically.
5. **Benchmark against the field's elite, not the average.** "Above the
   median" and "commensurate with peers at my rank" both fail; the
   standard is the small percentage at the very top. Name the comparison
   pool and beat it with sourced numbers.
6. **Per-article beats aggregate.** For original contributions, flagship
   works carry their OWN citation counts, citing institutions, and
   quotes; aggregate totals were expressly rejected in 2026 AAO
   decisions.
7. **No field-definition drift.** The frozen field phrase appears
   verbatim in every document; the criteria list is identical everywhere.
   A shifting denominator reads as a gamed field.
8. **Close the sustained-acclaim gap.** Recognition must span ≥2-3
   distinct career periods; an all-last-2-years record is a documented
   dismissal pattern. Early-career cases argue trajectory + current
   standing with field-percentile data — or wait.
9. **Independence audit.** For every exhibit ask "does the source have a
   stake?" Employer adoption, university press releases, and advisor
   letters are corroboration only; internal adoption presented as field
   acclaim is a documented failure mode.
10. **Quantitative anchor rule.** No paragraph without a sourced number
    (acceptance rate, percentile, selectivity ratio, per-article count).
    Replace all adjective clusters.
11. **Predatory-venue screen.** Any pay-to-publish journal, purchased
    judging, or citation-mill signature is now an affirmative liability
    (2025-26 revocation wave) — exclude it entirely.
12. **Date-classify everything; the frame is frozen once filed.**
    Eligibility is judged as of the filing date; post-filing achievements
    never establish it (documents created later that evidence pre-filing
    facts do), nothing new can be added on appeal, and the frozen field
    definition is never reworded in any response.

## Claim-verification log (run alongside the twelve rules at Stage V)

Extract every factual claim from every document (start from
documents/source-registry.md), verify each against its source, and log a
table: claim | source (exhibit / case.json / URL) | verified? | finding.
Severity: CRITICAL = contradicts the source (canonical example: "50K
monthly users" where the source says 50K total) · WARNING = unsupported —
REQUIRES SOURCE · INFO. A verified source is not the end of it: for every
load-bearing claim, read the registry's independent-verifier cell too. An
interested party (employer, investor, the applicant) vouching for a claim
is SELF-SERVING ONLY — one independent attestation now is far cheaper than
a final-merits finding later. The two most common failures get explicit passes:
dates consistent across ALL documents; titles consistent across resume, HR
letter, and forms. Close with a verdict line: READY / NEEDS FIXES / MAJOR
ISSUES. Approach with skepticism, not trust.

## If an RFE or NOID arrives

Switch to `rfe-response.md` and run stages R1–R7 (intake and the deadline
clock · diagnosis into rfe/response-plan.md · the evidence matrix and supply
loop · letters plan · supplemental statement · assembly and submission ·
the optional anonymous data point). Two rules from this file survive into the
response and are worth repeating there: the frozen field definition is never
reworded, and eligibility is judged as of the filing date.
