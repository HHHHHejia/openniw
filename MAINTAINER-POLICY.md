# Maintainer policy — why we will not help with your case

OpenNIW is freely published software. The people who maintain it are not
attorneys, are not your representatives, and **do not work on anyone's
individual immigration case** — not for a fee, not for free, not as a
favour, and not in exchange for a donation, a star, or a data-point
contribution.

This is not unfriendliness. Answering "does my case qualify?" or "how
should I word this?" for a specific person is the thing that would turn a
software project into something else entirely, and it is the one line that
the maintainers of this project will not cross. Please do not ask us to.

## What we will gladly help with

Open a GitHub issue for any of these:

- Installation, upgrades, and anything that errors out
- Bugs, crashes, wrong output, broken links
- Questions about how a feature works, or what a script does
- Where a number in the benchmark came from, and how it was computed
- Form field mappings, checklist entries, and citations to USCIS sources
  that look stale, wrong, or out of date — **please** report these
- Feature requests, new categories, translations
- Anything about the code, the licence, or the data

## What we will decline, every time

We will not answer, in public or in private, by any channel:

- "Do I qualify?" · "Is my profile strong enough?" · "What are my chances?"
- "Should I file?" · "Which category should I file in?" · "Should I file
  now or wait?"
- "Is this evidence enough?" · "Which of these should I submit?"
- "How should I word my endeavor / my letter / my statement?"
- "How do I answer this question on the form?"
- "How should I respond to this RFE?"
- "Can you look at my CV / draft / RFE notice / denial and tell me what you
  think?"

We will not accept or review your CV, your draft documents, your RFE
notice, your petition, or any other case material. If you send them, we
will not read them. Do not post case documents in a public issue — they
contain personal information you cannot take back.

**None of this changes if you offer to pay, donate, or sponsor.** There is
no tier of support that includes case help, because case help is not
something we provide at any price.

## If you need someone to look at your actual case

You need a licensed immigration attorney, or a
[DOJ-accredited representative](https://www.justice.gov/eoir/recognition-and-accreditation-program)
at a recognized non-profit organization. These are the people who are
permitted to advise you and who carry the responsibility that comes with
it. The software will tell you the same thing whenever it reaches a
question it should not answer.

## Contributing a data point

The [anonymized data-point form](https://github.com/HHHHHejia/openniw/issues/new?template=data-point.yml)
asks only for de-identified numbers and an outcome. Contributing one does
not create any relationship with the maintainers and does not entitle
anyone to case help — and we are grateful for it regardless.

## For contributors

If you contribute code, documentation, or playbook content, the same rule
applies to you when acting for this project: describe how the software
works, never advise a user on their own case. Pull requests that add
features asserting a legal conclusion — that someone qualifies, that
evidence is sufficient, that a filing is likely to be approved — will not
be merged. See the design rules in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).
