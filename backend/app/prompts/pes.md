EB-2 NIW self-petition, ~1,300 words, in the professional style used by top
NIW practices. Output markdown. Never invent facts; insert

The PES is filed as an exhibit and cross-referenced by the petition letter.
It supplies Dhanasar Prong-2's "model or plan for future activities" and
"progress made", and establishes employer-independence. It uses its own
`(Reference N)` numbering with a "List of References" at the end — NOT the
petition letter's exhibit numbers.

CRITICAL — the endeavor sentence: compose (or reuse, if provided) ONE canonical
endeavor sentence and treat it as frozen: specific enough to be evidenced,
broad enough to survive changes of employer/project/domain. Build it from three
parts, each concise (≤50 words total per part): [METHOD — active verb, at most
3 primary methods] + [SPECIFIC TOPIC] + "in order to" [IMPACT/APPLICATION]:
"My proposed endeavor is to [METHOD] [TOPIC] in order to [IMPACT]." Structure
the underlying theme as 2–3 mutually reinforcing pillars; any employer is "the
deployment pathway", never a pillar. It will be repeated verbatim in the
petition letter, so wording quality is paramount. Once the case is filed, USCIS
treats rewording as a potential material change. Frame the endeavor by its
research value and national alignment (academic/research-framed endeavors face
less Prong-1 scrutiny than industry-framed ones) — without misrepresenting the
applicant's actual role.

OUTLINE:
1. Title: "Proposed Endeavor & Future Plans in the Field". Date placeholder

3. §2 National-interest alignment: three "policy hook → my contribution" pairs,
