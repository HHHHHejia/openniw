# RFE Anatomy & Prevention Playbook (de-identified analysis)

Derived from a real all-prong NIW RFE and its successful response preparation.
This drives the evaluation heuristics, drafting rules, and the citation
pipeline in the backend. No personal information.

## Anatomy of the RFE

- Advanced-degree classification accepted; all three Dhanasar prongs challenged.
- Core finding: "the entire record consists of the petitioner's education,
  published or presented work, citation record, and employment information" —
  i.e. the record was credential-shaped, not endeavor-shaped.
- Prong 2 attacks observed: (1) credentials "common among researchers in the
  field" (an improper comparative test); (2) authorship position discounted by
  misreading a public scholar profile; (3) grant participation reduced to a
  share-of-output ratio; (4) employer claims block-quoted back and held
  unsubstantiated; (5) foreign affiliations + "requires substantial resources".
- Prong 3: four flat "did not" findings — nothing in the record addressed it.

## Response architecture that worked

Supplemental Personal Statement (6 sections) + 6 recommendation letters
(dependent/independent split) + testimonial letters (factual attestations:
employer/CEO, investors) + methodical evidence packages, each with an
index-of-exhibits that QUOTES the RFE sentence each exhibit answers.

Attorney's strategic read: abandon macro industry-level argumentation; argue
national importance from the concrete project itself along five dimensions:
**real-world need → application scenario → implementation path → beneficiaries
→ quantifiable impact**, plus a sixth: **means of execution** (funding,
personnel, entity, compute, collaborators). Adjudication climate: reviewers
focus almost entirely on executability of specific projects.

## The citation-example doctrine

- **HOW > WHO**: how the work was used matters more than who used it.
  Emphasizing citer prestige actively HARMS the case (invites credential
  comparison).
- Four high-value citation types: implemented / compared-favorably / utilized /
  verified. Low-value: passing background mentions. NEVER use citations that
  note limitations or where the citing work surpasses yours.
- Independence = no shared author between citing and cited paper (family name +
  first initial; escalate same-surname collisions).
- Only formally published citing papers qualify (no preprints, posters,
  under-review records, retitled duplicates).
- Verify each claimed citation actually appears in the citing full text
  (observed false-positive rate: ~5% of indexed citations).
- Select for portfolio coverage across cited papers and subfields; overlap the
  selection with candidate independent recommenders so letters and exhibits
  corroborate each other.
- Deliverable: numbered, highlighted PDFs (in-text citation + reference entry
  only) + structured summary (background, verbatim quote, objectives,
  importance, how/why used, findings — with prescribed sentence counts).

## Recommender doctrine

- Dependent letters (advisors/collaborators): first-hand knowledge → Prong 1
  (endeavor's national importance) + Prong 2. Attribute specific technical
  contributions (rebuts ratio arithmetic).
- Independent letters: must have cited the work; discuss why they cited it AND
  reference ≥1–2 OTHER notable citations (≥2 total citations each, else weak).
- Prefer U.S.-based recommenders in different states; established experts only,
  no students. Recommender bio/CV must NOT include their citation/publication
  counts (invites unfair comparison).
- Letters from government or quasi-governmental entities are the highest-value,
  most-overlooked class — can support all three prongs.

## Twelve rules for the INITIAL filing (RFE prevention)

1. **Endeavor as executable project, not research area.** Validate six fields
   per project: need, scenario, implementation path, beneficiaries,
   quantifiable impact, means of execution. Never argue from field importance.
2. **No uncorroborated third-party claims.** Parse drafts for factual claims
   about entities; each must bind to an exhibit or be deleted (not softened).
   The officer will quote your most impressive unsupported sentence back.
3. **Pre-empt "common among researchers".** Degrees/pubs/citations/reviews are
   baseline credentials; require ≥1 differentiating item (implementation by
   others, inbound collaboration requests, investment, government interest,
   critical role attested first-hand). Pre-load the doctrinal rebuttal: Prong 2
   is not a comparative test.
4. **Assert authorship position and honor scope explicitly with primary proof.**
   Highlight equal-contribution notations in the official published version.
   State award selectivity as a ratio. Derive first anything an officer could
   derive from a public profile — and derive it correctly.
5. **Never expose a denominator.** Describe funded-research involvement as
   "participated in projects funded by [award]" with a first-hand
   contribution-quality attestation; never as grantee, never in ratio terms.
6. **Foreign affiliation = mandatory documentation trigger**: proof of
   affiliation + where work is physically performed (remotely from the U.S.) +
   a resources answer (runs on standard infrastructure). Unify endeavor under
   one theme with a U.S. delivery vehicle.
7. **Answer "benefits only your employer" affirmatively**: dissemination
   argument (publications, patents' public disclosure, open source); decouple
   national significance from commercial scale.
8. **Build Prong 3 from facts, not adjectives**, covering all five balancing
   factors (impracticality, benefit despite available workers, urgency
   [distinct from importance], job creation, self-employment factor).
9. **Run the citation pipeline at filing time** (harvest → verify existence →
   independence → depth scoring → negative-citation quarantine → published-only
   filter → portfolio selection → highlighted PDFs + summary).
10. **Global consistency layer**: one canonical fact table (venues, years,
    authorship, counts + as-of dates, employment terms, entity facts, funding);
    validate every artifact against it; re-validate letters at signature time;
    scan all case documents (incl. marketing materials) for contradictions.
11. **Date-classify every exhibit** vs. the priority date: pre-filing /
    post-filing-continuation / post-filing-discard; label indexes accordingly;
    flag claims resting on nothing pre-filing.
12. **The endeavor sentence is frozen once filed.** Compose it once,
    defensively: specific enough to evidence, broad enough to survive employer/
    project changes; 2–3 pillars under one theme; employer as deployment
    pathway; no topics without an execution vehicle; ≥50% research framing for
    industry-employed applicants; in-text numbered references weighted toward
    U.S. government sources (market-size reports alone are the weakest class).

## Meta-observation (drives OpenNIW's architecture)

The most reusable artifacts are the CONTROL FILES, not the prose: per-citation
scan reports with scores + quoted context, rejection logs with reasons,
verification audits, exhibit indexes that quote what they answer, letter-package
summaries describing each letter by the rebuttal it performs. OpenNIW treats
these structured control artifacts as primary outputs and assembles the
narrative documents from them — the inverse of traditional petition prep.
